Kirana Voice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Kirana Voice ("the app") is published by Naresh Podichetty. Contact: podichettynaresh@gmail.com.

Summary

Your shop's business records — sales, bills, stock, prices, purchases, money collected, customers, dues, your address and GSTIN — are stored only on your own device. We do not receive them, and the app turns off the phone's automatic cloud backup for them.

What stays on your device

  • Products, prices and stock; bills, payments, credit and returns; purchases and distributors.
  • Your customers' names, phone numbers and what they owe (khata). Payment reminders and statements are opened in WhatsApp (or another app you pick) and sent by you; we never send messages to your customers and never receive their details.
  • Shop details you type in (shop name, address, GSTIN, phone).
  • Backups you create. If you set a backup password, backups are encrypted (AES-256). You choose where to send a backup (for example your own Google Drive or email); we never receive it.
  • Your app-lock PIN, kept only as a one-way hash in the phone's secure keystore.

What we receive

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your shop's data to us or to anyone else. The app does not need the internet for billing, stock or khata; it uses the internet only when you choose to (for example for online speech recognition or to share a file).

Voice

When you tap the microphone, your speech is sent to your phone's speech-recognition service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone, Microsoft on Windows, or your browser's) to turn it into text. That service may process audio on its servers under its own privacy policy. The app does not record or store audio, and only the recognised text is used, on your device.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you are speaking a command.
  • Bluetooth: only to connect to your receipt printer.
  • Notifications: only for low-stock, backup and payment-due reminders you turn on.
  • Fingerprint / face: only to unlock the app; handled by the phone, never seen by the app.

The app does not access your contacts, location, photos or files, except files you choose to share, export or restore.

Sharing and messages

When you share a bill, report or order, or send an order on WhatsApp or SMS, the app hands the content to the app you choose. What happens next is up to that app and the person you send it to.

Children

The app is for shop owners and is not directed at children under 18.

Your choices

  • Delete data on your device: uninstall the app, or use Remove demo data and the app's own screens. Make a backup first if you want to keep your records.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.
