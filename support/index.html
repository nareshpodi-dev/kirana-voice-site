Kirana Voice — Support

Need help with Kirana Voice? Write to podichettynaresh@gmail.com. Please mention your phone model, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what you were doing.

Common questions

Voice does not understand me. Check the speech language in Settings → Voice & microphone. Some languages need internet or a downloaded language pack on the phone. If you speak slowly or repeat words, choose a slower speaking pace in Settings → Voice & microphone. You can always tap Type instead.

Is my data online? No. Sales, stock, prices and customers stay on your phone. Make backups from Settings → Create backup and keep them somewhere safe; you can protect them with a password.

How do I move to a new phone? Make a backup on the old phone, send it to yourself, and use Restore from backup file in Settings on the new phone.

Bluetooth printer does not connect. Turn the printer on, pair it in the phone's Bluetooth settings, then choose it in Settings → Bluetooth printer. 58 mm and 80 mm ESC/POS printers are supported.

WhatsApp reminders. The app opens WhatsApp with the message ready; you press Send. The app never sends messages by itself.
